03 — How a project runs06

From first call
to launch — and after.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    You tell us the idea. We ask a lot of questions. No commitment, no pitch deck needed.

  2. 02

    Discovery

    We map the problem, the people and the risks — then agree on scope, plan and budget.

  3. 03

    Design

    Flows, prototypes and the look and feel, tested before a single feature is built.

  4. 04

    Build

    We work in short cycles and show you working software as we go.

  5. 05

    Launch

    We ship it, watch how it’s used and fix what needs fixing.

  6. 06

    Grow

    Support, improvements and new features — for as long as you need us.

At every step, you talk to the people doing the work.

07 — Questions10

The things people
usually ask.

What does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After the first conversation you get a clear estimate — no surprises later.

How long does it take?

A prototype takes weeks; a full product takes months. You get a real timeline after discovery.

Do I need a detailed brief?

No. An idea and a few sentences are enough to start.

Can you work with what we have?

Yes — an existing codebase, design system or team. Or we start fresh.

Will you sign an NDA?

Of course. Your idea stays yours.

What happens after launch?

We stay on for support and new features, if you want us to.

Something else?
Just ask.

08 — Start a project11

Starting
is simple.

Send us a few lines about

  • Who it’s for
  • What exists already
